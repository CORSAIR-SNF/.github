## 🎯 About

**CORSAIR** is a PhD project devoted to the writings of **Lancelot Voisin de La Popelinière** (1541-1608), a French Reformed historian. It aims to understand how his historiographical narrative is constructed by studying the **intertextual links** he maintained with his Protestant and Catholic contemporaries: plagiarism, borrowing, rewriting, allusions.

To this end, we are developing a **digital humanities tool** based on **word embeddings**. It automates the search for **semantic correspondences** between two texts, and thereby sheds light on this singular author's politics of borrowing in the development of the discipline of history.

## 🧭 Research areas

- **History and historiography**: historical narrative in the age of the Wars of Religion
- **Intertextuality**: sources, borrowings and rewritings among Protestant and Catholic authors
- **Veridiction**: how historical discourse builds its authority and claim to truth
- **Digital humanities**: automatic detection of semantic similarities between texts
